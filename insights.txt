1. Technology is the highest revenue-generating category, consistently outperforming Furniture and Office Supplies.
Across all regions, the Technology category leads in total sales, contributing the largest share to company revenue.
 This is likely due to the higher unit prices and growing demand for electronic devices and accessories. Not only are sales higher, but profit 
margins in Technology are also among the strongest, indicating both volume and profitability advantages.

2. The West region is the most profitable and dominant in terms of overall sales.
The dashboard reveals that the West region outpaces other regions in both sales and profit. 
This suggests either a larger customer base, more strategic product targeting, or better regional marketing performance. 
This region could serve as a model for others — potentially offering insights into successful product-category combinations or promotional strategies.

3. Sales show strong seasonal trends, with peaks occurring around November and December.
A clear spike in the line chart occurs toward the end of the year, especially in November, 
which aligns with holiday shopping periods like Black Friday and Cyber Monday. This seasonal trend 
could inform inventory planning and marketing efforts for Q4. Conversely, the slowest months 
(e.g., February or April) may offer opportunities for targeted promotions to lift off-season sales.

4. Office Supplies generate moderate sales but consistently yield the lowest profit margins.
While Office Supplies contribute a reasonable amount to sales volume, their profitability is noticeably 
lower than other categories. This indicates either higher costs, lower markups, or over-discounting in 
this category. It may be worth reviewing pricing strategy or supplier costs to improve margin performance in this segment.
